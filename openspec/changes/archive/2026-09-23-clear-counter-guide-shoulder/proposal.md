## Why

The operating Curta's counter carry slider enters the unchanged guide's upper
ledge at its full 4.2 mm stroke: .5953499945 mm³ native and .5953568840 mm³
world64 at the first station. The localized .30 mm shoulder penetration is
recorded in `simulation/docs/carry-guide-contact-2026-09-23.md`; it is separate
from the already-cleared frame passages and nominal guide-face tangencies.

## What Changes

- Trial a strictly bounded underside relief of the counter slider's outer
  shoulder, keeping the guide, stroke, placement and source assets unchanged.
- Prove removal confinement, connected material and preservation of spring
  detents, guide-running faces, reset shoe, pin tip and transmission fork.
- Check the localized shoulder interface at all five counter stations through
  the full stroke, with source and insufficient-relief negative controls.
- Adopt only after those scoped checks pass and production state/geometry
  preservation is demonstrated. Report remaining guide tangencies and other
  neighbours separately, without overlap allowances or whole-guide acceptance.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `counter-guide-shoulder-clearance`: A full-stroke counter shoulder passes the
  unchanged guide ledge while preserving its other physical interfaces.

### Modified Capabilities

None. This bounded fit does not complete or weaken the whole-machine specs.

## Impact

Project-owned counter-slider fitting layer, isolated all-station fixture,
tests and evidence. No framework/viewer API, operating law, control, source
STEP/STL or manufacturing recommendation changes. Routine measured trial
work proceeds under the pilot's standing autonomous implementation authority;
failure of a protected interface rejects the trial rather than broadening it.
