## Purpose

Let the counter carry shoulders traverse their full stroke past the fixed guide
ledges while preserving working interfaces and keeping other contact findings explicit.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: The counter shoulder clears its unchanged guide ledge

Each operating counter carry shoulder SHALL complete its original 4.2 mm
stroke without penetrating the unchanged guide's upper ledge. A scoped relief
SHALL preserve the original source assets, installed placement and travel.
Native verification and published-mesh evidence SHALL agree on the named
ledge interface; refused verification SHALL NOT count as clearance.

#### Scenario: Full counter stroke
- **WHEN** any of the five counter sliders travels from rest to full drop
- **THEN** its shoulder clears the unchanged guide ledge through the stroke
- **AND** the unrelieved source and insufficient-relief negative controls
  retain a measurable positive endpoint obstruction

### Requirement: The shoulder relief preserves the other interfaces

Removal SHALL stay inside the independently bounded shoulder underside region.
The slider SHALL remain one valid connected solid and retain its guide-running
faces, spring detents, reset shoe, transmission fork and pin-contact tip.
The fit SHALL change no other part, retained coordinate or operating law.

#### Scenario: Compare source and fitted mechanisms
- **WHEN** source and fitted sliders and their installed mechanisms are compared
- **THEN** no material is added and material outside the allowed region is identical
- **AND** the protected interfaces and their existing free/blocked contracts pass
- **AND** unchanged input sequences produce the same retained coordinate banks

### Requirement: Scoped clearance does not waive other guide findings

The report SHALL distinguish the cleared ledge from other guide contacts and
retention findings. It SHALL NOT excuse a positive common with a threshold,
subtract a nominal contact, or describe this fit as whole-machine acceptance.

#### Scenario: The complete guide still has a separate contact finding
- **WHEN** the localized shoulder passes but another guide interface is unresolved
- **THEN** the remaining measured finding stays explicit and unaccepted
