## Context

Project main `d6f298f` retains the earlier two-station `CarryPassageFrame`.
The remaining result stations have native raised/lowered frame commons near
4.626578/.099225 mm³. Counter stations have different commons near
3.370756/3.741717 mm³; they are not assumed to be rotated result stations.
All fifteen sampled mid-stroke slider positions clear. The 45 read-only
witnesses preserve the entire initial bank and use each joint site's parent
frame, not the imported leaf's rotated frame. An earlier leaf-frame diagnostic
is invalid for stroke clearance and remains recorded as such.

This plan exercises the pilot's standing autonomous completion authority.
It does not extend the historical two-station approval by assertion: each new
station requires its own measured permitted and protected regions before any
production cut. The accepted first-pair proof remains a regression gate.

## Goals / Non-Goals

Goals:

- Clear the complete supported 4.2 mm travel of all ten result and five counter
  carry stations against the installed fixed frame, including their springs.
- Preserve existing sliders, guides, spring seats, hooks, fork/sleeve capture,
  pins, reset interfaces and operating timing.
- Establish bounded, independently dimensioned local frame removal and retain
  passing source-preservation, full-path and installed-root evidence.

Non-goals: moving-part changes; a force/strain or strength model; new controls;
framework/viewer changes; source-file edits; clearing unrelated rest or moving
contacts by exemption; final whole-machine or manufacturing certification.

## Decisions

### Measure each station before using a repeated fit

Use the actual installed native bodies and world64 meshes. Record the raised,
lowered, preload and detent-extremum contacts, parent-frame axis, source-frame
transforms, original guide/frame contact lands and neighbouring fastener,
shaft, nut and spring-support features. A reused cutter family is permitted
only after independent transformed evidence supports it. Counter shape and
placement are measured separately, not inferred from rotational symmetry.

### Keep the existing moving mechanism and fit only the fixed frame

Prefer local stationary passage removal with the existing .05 mm named gap.
Do not remove a whole annular band, use a whole-station box, move the guide,
trim a slider or alter a motion law. Such alternatives change already measured
interfaces or conceal the obstruction. Keep the original two-station adapter
available for paired comparison and preserve its existing tests.

Dimension permitted removal from source contacts and swept envelopes before
constructing production cutters. Permit only local frame-side seat-edge
strips analogous to the first pair where independent measurements establish
their boundaries and retained support. Use the historical .08 mm maximum
edge envelope as a conservative initial bound, not a license to enlarge it.
Measure actual removed/retained land area; preserve the rest of each native
land exactly and at least the first-pair precedent of 98% of the mapped land.
If this fails, or any fastening, support bridge, shaft/bearing feature or
moving part must change, report the specific conflict before broadening the
design. No claim of structural adequacy follows from geometric continuity.

### Prove preserved material and full travel independently

First add named red tests for every new station. Fit tests require one valid
connected solid, no added material, zero removal outside independently
defined regions, unchanged protected geometry/contact and unchanged other
parts and placements. Test omission/misplacement of a passage, excessive
removal, hidden frame and altered stroke as negative controls. The .04–.06 mm
trial gap range from the earlier fit remains bounded and must measurably
change the intended clearance without damaging protected lands.

Dense native/world64 sampling includes at least 41 stroke positions, the
detent knots/extrema and preload. Also bound the unsampled slider/spring
travel using the existing swept/interval methods, adapting their frame and
station selection without substituting samples for that proof. Compare fresh
and built geometry so cached CAD cannot hide a changed fit. Clearance must
hold against the complete frame, with no volume epsilon or omitted obstacle.

Check source-preserved guide play, spring seating/capture and fork/sleeve
engagement alongside clearance. Replay actual retained carry/reset scenarios
in the complete operating root; record unrelated known contacts separately,
not as local passes or reasons to alter timing. Inspect sections of every
distinct fit family and an assembled view with supports present.

## Risks / Trade-offs

- Repeated-looking stations may differ → independent native measurements and
  per-station protected-region checks precede cutter reuse.
- Seat clearance may require excessive loss of support → the measured .08 mm
  edge bound and 98% retained-land precedent are stop gates, not adjustable
  thresholds chosen after seeing a failure.
- Dense samples miss a contact → retain an independent conservative full-path
  bound and leave the interface unfinished if it cannot be established.
- World64 and native source shapes differ → record both; no precision swap
  or small-positive-volume waiver.
- Concurrent framework work changes results → pin test imports to a recorded
  verified worktree and rerun affected operating checks after integration.

## Migration Plan

Validate and commit this planning record with its read-only evidence. Add
red contracts, measure safe regions, implement the minimal stationary fit,
verify both kernels and actual operating regressions, then sync/archive only
this completed capability. Keep the broader `simulate-the-curta` tasks open
until their independent obligations pass. Rollback is a focused normal revert,
not resetting user work or deleting source artifacts. No push or publication.
