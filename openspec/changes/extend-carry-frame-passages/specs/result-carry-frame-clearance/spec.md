## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: A local frame fit preserves the rest of the assembly

The corrected frame SHALL remain one valid connected solid and preserve its
guide-support, fastening, main-shaft, bearing and other station features.
The first two stations MAY have documented local frame-side relief at the
raised/lowered slider-contact seat edges, provided their remaining guide
registration lands retain their original geometry and supporting contact.
The guides themselves and their placement SHALL remain unchanged.
Only documented local passage regions SHALL differ from the original frame:
the verified first two result stations and any additional station separately
verified under the carry-bank frame-clearance requirements. Additional relief
SHALL NOT weaken the first pair's existing clearance or preservation contracts.
Upstream source files and attribution SHALL remain unchanged.

#### Scenario: Inspect the corrected frame

- **WHEN** the maker inspects the frame and its mounted assemblies after fitting
- **THEN** the two selected stations have the documented local running gaps
- **AND** support seats outside the documented seat-edge exception, fasteners,
  shaft alignment and stations without a separately verified local passage
  retain their original geometry and placement
- **AND** no material is added or removed outside the documented passage regions

#### Scenario: Inspect a relieved guide-seat edge

- **WHEN** a selected slider passage needs clearance at its guide-seat boundary
- **THEN** only the documented frame-side edge region is relieved
- **AND** the remaining registration land preserves its source geometry and
  supporting contact with the unchanged guide
- **AND** the removed and retained seating regions are independently measured
