## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Complete carry-bank movement clears the fixed frame

The simulation SHALL preserve the full supported carry/reset travel at all ten
result carry stations and all five counter carry stations while keeping their
sliders and springs clear of the complete installed frame. The frame and
supports SHALL remain present and stationary.

#### Scenario: Traverse any installed carry station

- **WHEN** any result or counter carry station moves between its two detents
  through its supported 4.2 mm travel and preload positions
- **THEN** its slider and spring do not penetrate the complete frame
- **AND** neither shortened travel, hidden material nor altered carry/reset
  timing substitutes for clearance

### Requirement: Bank fitting preserves the supported mechanism

The corrected frame SHALL remain one valid connected solid. Its local passage
reliefs SHALL preserve the source guide placement, fastening and shaft/bearing
features, spring seats, retaining contacts and transmitted-motion interfaces.
Only independently documented local frame-side contact regions SHALL change;
no moving part or upstream source file SHALL be changed for this correction.

#### Scenario: Inspect a fitted carry station

- **WHEN** a maker inspects any fitted station throughout its supported travel
- **THEN** its original guide and remaining registration lands still support
  the slider, its spring remains seated and captured, and the fork remains
  engaged with its coupled sleeve
- **AND** local frame-side seat-edge relief does not remove or relocate a guide,
  fastening feature, support bridge or other protected interface

#### Scenario: Detect an excessive or incomplete passage

- **WHEN** a passage is omitted, misplaced or extended into protected material
- **THEN** the affected clearance or material-preservation contract fails
- **AND** a smaller positive volume does not convert the failure into a pass

### Requirement: Evidence distinguishes local completion from the whole machine

The simulation SHALL retain independent source-preservation and full-travel
evidence for each fitted station, with unresolved findings reported separately
from verified local clearance.

#### Scenario: Complete the carry-bank frame correction

- **WHEN** all fifteen carry/frame interfaces meet their clearance and
  preservation requirements
- **THEN** the record identifies the corrected stations, permitted removal,
  retained supports, passing operating checks and inspected geometry
- **AND** unrelated whole-machine contacts and final viewer acceptance remain
  open unless separately verified
