# Five turns carry springs — made-wire evidence

Marcus Wu's `Manual/Curta Build Manual.pdf`, printed page 30, gives five
turns carry levers alongside ten result carry levers. Their springs use
about 57 mm of 0.6 mm music wire each, bent with the carry-spring tool.
The [hardware BOM](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/16EJePozXW-uC6UFISzyT2eMk7c8wh6v-EP5L1U8fzfM/edit#gid=1182246287)
lists fifteen made springs in total, not fifteen for each bank.

The five actual wire leaves remain explicitly unassigned until a supported
made-wire process exists. No supplier pack allocation, measured mass or
material density is inferred. Source inspected 2026-10-04.
