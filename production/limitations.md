# Partial Curta3x production — draft limitations

This asset binds the existing `simulation.mechanistic:MechanisticCurta`.
It accounts for four crank printed units and seven DIN 934 M4x0.7 nuts.
It leaves all other candidates unassigned, including fifteen made-wire
carry springs. Printed polymer and all finished masses remain unknown.

The current model includes an explicitly simulation-only captive clearing-loop
mounting. Whole-machine geometry acceptance remains open. Two crank parts
already carry simulation fit replacements. Adding production changes none
of that geometry and does not substitute author geometry silently.

The retaining spring is one author printed item represented by five
rigid/flexible patches. Its finished-part boundary remains unresolved.
The author hardware sheet also has conflicting decimal-marker ball entries;
the ball rows are not blindly summed or assigned. The assembly manual's
carriage-spring page is unfinished. These records do not claim a complete
reconciled BOM, printability, installed proof, fabrication readiness or a
supplier shopping list. Mesh counts, STEP ingredients and markings are
not author finished-piece quantities.

Sources: Marcus Wu's [3x BOM](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/16EJePozXW-uC6UFISzyT2eMk7c8wh6v-EP5L1U8fzfM/edit),
the local `Manual/Curta Build Manual.pdf`, and this project's existing
simulation source at revision `fb4708e98463b3cb4c0130cc3e92514c2a31155b`.
Evidence inspected 2026-10-04. This text is a project-owned synthesis of
attributed evidence and does not reproduce the complete manual.
