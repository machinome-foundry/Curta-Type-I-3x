# Crank assembly — draft source notes

Marcus Wu's local `Manual/Curta Build Manual.pdf`, printed pages 49–50,
describes tapping the handle with M4, threading its pin screw with an M4
die, and preparing externally visible crank parts before assembly.
The [author assembly video](https://youtu.be/zh2Z11miQ0w) is supplementary evidence.

This profile accounts for four handled units: main crank, handle, pin
screw and crank handle pin. It retains the two existing simulation fit
replacements identified by the preceding instruction. Assembly notes do
not certify those replacements or the complete calculator for fabrication.

Attributed to Marcus Wu; source inspected 2026-10-04.
